# PRODUCT.md

netuqo is a radically simple personal focus and work-memory system for one person.

**Tagline:** Simply know what's next.

**Core loop:** Capture → Focus → Done → Remember.

netuqo knows a lot, and shows only what matters right now. Complexity is allowed to grow
inside the system. It must never reach the user interface.

## Target user

Primary ICP: Geschäftsführer, Founder, Unternehmer, vertriebsnahe Führungskräfte — people
with high personal responsibility, heavy email/calendar load, and no interest in maintaining
a productivity system. Single-user by design.

## What netuqo is

- A personal focus layer over Mail, Calendar, Microsoft 365, and later further sources.
- One calm place for "what matters now."
- A durable personal work memory: completed work stays valuable and searchable.
- A product that requires less operation as it gets smarter — never more.

## What netuqo explicitly is not

- Not an Asana/Jira/Planner replacement.
- Not a team workspace.
- Not a dashboard builder.
- Not a CRM, ERP, or wiki.
- Not "an AI chatbot with a task list."

## Kernbotschaften

- Simply know what's next.
- Know a lot. Show little.
- Less managing. More doing.
- Everything important is under control.
- Done matters as much as To Do.
